# S*x*mo: Support

We have the following support channels for questions and bug reports:

* [sxmo-devel Mailing List](https://lists.sr.ht/%7Emil/sxmo-devel) - This is our primary asynchronous **developer** support channel where you can send your patches.
* [sxmo-user Mailing List](https://lists.sr.ht/%7Emil/sxmo-user) - This is our primary asynchronous **user** support channel where you can send questions, 
proposals for bug reports, and proposals for feature requests. Please check if your question is not already answered before posting. 
Be advised, the bug tracker is for confirmed bugs and features not user support. Here you can also discuss the general goals of
the project, your experience daily driving, and have other fun discussions with the Sxmo community.
* [IRC Channel](irc://irc.oftc.net/#sxmo) - We have a channel ``#sxmo`` and ``#sxmo-offtopic`` on the [OFTC network](https://oftc.net). Connect with your favourite IRC client or use the [web-based chat](https://webchat.oftc.net/) if you prefer. Here you can talk with Sxmo users and developers.
