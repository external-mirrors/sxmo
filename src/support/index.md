# S*x*mo: Support

We have the following support channels for questions and bug reports:

* [sxmo-devel Mailing List](https://lists.sr.ht/%7Emil/sxmo-devel) - This is our primary asynchronous **user** and ****developer** support channel where you can send your questions, proposals for bug reports and proposals for feature requests. Please check if your question is not already answered before posting. Be advised, the bug tracker is for confirmed bugs and features not user support.
* [IRC Channel](irc://irc.oftc.net/#sxmo) - We have a channel ``#sxmo`` and ``#sxmo-offtopic`` on the [OFTC network](https://oftc.net). Connect with your favourite IRC client or use the [web-based chat](https://webchat.oftc.net/) if you prefer. Here you can talk with Sxmo users and developers.
