# Downloading S*x*mo

Sxmo is shipped by [postmarketOS](https://postmarketos.org), a real linux
distribution for phones and other mobile devices. PostmarketOS is in turn based
on [Alpine Linux](https://alpinelinux.org).

PostmarketOS offers pre-baked images which you can flash to your device.
Please follow our [installation guide](https://man.sr.ht/~anjan/sxmo-docs-stable/INSTALLGUIDE.md#strongprebaked-imagesstrong) for further instructions.

Are you running Arch Arm instead? Then check out [sxmo-alarm](https://github.com/justinesmithies/sxmo-alarm) to get
sxmo running on your distribution.
